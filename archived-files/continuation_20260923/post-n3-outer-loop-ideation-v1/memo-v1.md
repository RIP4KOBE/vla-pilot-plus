## Four idea moves and three distinct alternatives

1. **Assumption inversion:** search archive memory need not be the execution pool. This produces the recommended terminal-population consumer.
2. **Backward from success:** preserving coordinated gripper/pose programs, rather than geometric endpoint equivalence, could retain manipulation options. This produces a temporal-command representation alternative, but available data do not yet identify which such programs matter.
3. **Analogical transfer:** distinguish a search frontier from the incumbent history, as in population/beam search; separately, receding-horizon plan continuity motivates a guarded tail-carry alternative. These analogies suggest mechanisms, not guarantees that later candidates or continued plans are better.
4. **Failure-case reverse engineering:** tasks 2/6 show high activity and better predictive association without completion; task 8 loses a control success. A consumer boundary is a concrete lever shared by these examples. They do not license changing Stage, selecting task-specific rules, clipping commands or fitting a residual gain.
