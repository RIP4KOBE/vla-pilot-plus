## Why no immediate new candidate is released

The overflow observation alone does not license another partition expansion: historical H1 drove post-expansion overflow to zero and still obtained only 1/10 validation versus 0/10 control, below its frozen gate. N2's full-prefix objective was 1/10 against Q0 1/10 and C0 2/10; N3's executed-feedback objective was 0/10 against Q0 1/10 and C0 3/10 despite verified selection activity. N4 now changes the execution pool and gains one exposed Dev task, yet misses its gate. These results make another scalar geometric-proxy refinement or stacking H1 with N4 unsupported.

The saved N4 native scoring inputs contain candidate latent tensors and predicted EEF trajectories, and the audit associates actual selected actions, but they do not contain physical outcomes of rejected candidates. Visual samples do not establish a candidate-specific contact failure. A new temporal-program descriptor or cross-chunk carry mechanism therefore lacks the exact task-linked loss/recovery witness required to select it over the other options. This review does not claim those mechanism classes are impossible.

## Resource and evidence boundary

The private N4/W budget is **92/240 dispatches** and **12.861807916615254/48 GPU hours**: baseline **20/20**, development **66/100**, recovery **6/20**, validation **0/60**, formal **0/40**. Protected validation/formal hours remain 14+10. There are 34 development slots but no baseline slots; a new contemporary control matrix would need its allocation prospectively frozen within the remaining development bucket, with no historical control numbers substituted for same-round results. The ten Dev task/seed/init identities are all exposed; `data/exposure-registry.jsonl` was reconciled from the append-only attempt ledger on 2026-09-24. Neither fresh validation nor the historical formal acceptance set was read in this review.

The next evidence question is whether the current QD/ED search **proposes an action that can move the task-relevant object from the same observed state, and whether selection rejects it**. The present artifacts cannot answer it. The execution prompt allows short same-state candidate rollouts only when complete state restoration is proved and their extra branch-simulation scope is separately approved. Such a diagnostic would be exploratory and would never enter official success-rate denominators or become deployed privileged input. A versioned, capped protocol and restoration preflight are required before seeking that scope. Until then, no new GPU work or ungrounded N5 is authorized by this review.
