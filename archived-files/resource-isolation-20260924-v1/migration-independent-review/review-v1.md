# Independent resource-isolation migration review

Decision: **PASS**, source/CPU preparation only. No real migration, real journal partition/preflight, GPU, model or online provider was executed by this reviewer. The exact source/test hashes and command are in `review-v1.json`.

The reviewed implementation verifies the actual old runner flock holder through `/proc/locks`, live process/boot/start identity, helper basename, old inode, a failing competing nonblocking flock, and bound drain evidence. It also holds the old W state lock. All episode/health groups must have legal complete phases and finite nonnegative recorded duration before preparing any destination replacement. Unknown owner or dangling retry ancestry fails closed.

Partitioning retains every original nonblank newline-terminated JSONL line byte-for-byte and in original owner-relative order. Every entire group is assigned once by its recorded output artifact root, with per-line/group hashes and source line numbers. Decimal resource-time and event/dispatch conservation are recorded. Cross-owner retry links remain unchanged and obtain lineage-only references into the immutable shared archive; the predecessor is not copied into another cost ledger. A nonempty reservation ledger is deliberately unsupported and stops before migration rather than guessing ownership.

W/X retain separate 240/48 and 250/48 budgets and their approved bucket caps/protected reserves. The initial worker-key typo was corrected to the actual runner field `max_concurrent_gpu_workers=1`; original run/artifact owner binding is now checked. W inherited X authority is removed; X's exact frozen N1W authority is retained with old reallocation metadata explicitly scoped to X. Old external holds/priority are superseded and X's shared-link enforcement is disabled through its resource state, without modifying runner code. States remain `MIGRATED_PENDING_VALIDATION`, not ACTIVE.

Original shared journals, both raw state files and old links/lease are archived before replacement. Both scientific HEADs, frozen plans, source files, profiles, runtime/inventory/seeds, N4 resource pointer/contract and X authority are checked unchanged before and after replacement. Required current owner health proof groups must survive partitioning. W runner/state locks are never replacement destinations. X's three journals and two locks become real owner files through individual atomic `os.replace` operations.

**Atomicity limit:** the whole migration is not one multi-file transaction. The old lease and no-new-supervisor operational rule protect the sequence. Prepared stage hashes, original archives and per-replacement progress allow manual recovery after interruption; the script refuses automatic rerun, rollback, activation or lease release. A SIGKILL between replacement and progress persistence requires inspecting destination hashes against the prepared manifest. Pre-replacement archive/staging failures may leave a partial archive but cannot have changed live destinations and are also blocked from blind rerun.

**CPU evidence:** 18 author targeted tests plus 6 independent synthetic checks passed together: **24 passed in 0.16 seconds**. Independent checks cover interleaved exact bytes, cross-owner lineage and cost conservation; unfinished/prefix-collision/dangling-owner refusal; owner state/hold preservation; and a second-replacement fault. The fault test runs `migrate()` only against entirely invented temporary roots, with external lease/source-inventory gates stubbed specifically to isolate filesystem failure mechanics. It proves the first replacement is listed, original archive/prepared/progress/partial remain, X links and the W lock remain, and no final migration/release appears. The separate author test uses a real temporary CPU helper and flock and rejects a forged/reused holder. Test counts overlap reviewed behavior and are not scientific or runtime performance evidence.

Root must still validate real private destinations and recomputed owner state, confirm independent lock inodes and unchanged scientific identities, then explicitly mark ACTIVE and release the held old lease. Neither this review nor the migration script authorizes dispatch. Historical cross-owner retry references preserve auditability, not permission for future cross-study retry/state access.
