# Continuity literature boundary note — 2026-09-30

This read-only literature note grants no algorithm implementation or active protocol change. Primary sources were inspected during the running N23 Dev cohort. Neither paper evaluates this frozen RDT/QD/ED checkpoint, task setup, Stage interface or budget.

[Real-Time Execution of Action Chunking Flow Policies, arXiv2506.07339v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07339v2), sections2–3: RTC uses inference-time inpainting to preserve actions committed during asynchronous inference and softly conditions later overlapping actions. Its guidance uses a vector-Jacobian product and clipping; the paper warns that averaging multimodal actions can generate poor actions. These results motivate examining boundary consistency, but do not establish that our unexecuted tails should be retained. Local inference: directly adopting its asynchronous execution and changing execution horizon would violate this study's frozen executor. Any isolated QD/EDS adaptation would need its own causal hypothesis, current-observation conditioning, reset/target invalidation and complete compute accounting. Our simulator's synchronized stepping also differs from its inference-delay motivation.

[STEP: Warm-Started Visuomotor Policies with Spatiotemporal Consistency Prediction, arXiv2602.08245v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08245v1), section3.2 and appendixA: STEP conditions an action predictor on current observation and previous actions; its predictor is trained (appendixA lists100,000 iterations). It also includes a velocity-based perturbation mechanism. Local inference: this is not an immediately usable training-free switch for this study. A new learned predictor would require an approved training/data/model scope extension. No paper success rates are imported as expected improvement here.

Local evidence remains the four-completed-attempt planned-continuity diagnostic and the separately bounded executed-proprioception follow-up. Same target/Stage identity does not imply unchanged observations, positive gripper commands do not prove grasp, and an unexecuted tail provides no physical counterfactual. No successor candidate has been selected by this note.
