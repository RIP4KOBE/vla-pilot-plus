## Reconciliation and stopping semantics

The map defines ten semantic task triples. Tasks0–3 are complete historical triples; task4 has retained C0/Q0 and one proposed recovery; tasks5–9 are untouched proposed triples. Reconciliation must verify each included terminal's own original plan, release, manifest, native and generic hashes before union. It must reject duplicate attempts, duplicate semantic occupants, cross-profile mappings, unapproved condition changes, and invalid/unknown terminal inclusion. Exact execution identity and scientific comparison identity are different fields, not interchangeable aliases.

Only complete valid C0/Q0/N32 triples enter futility: `N32 successes + (10 - complete valid triples) < 5`. At the current four complete triples there are no successes and six possible candidate cells left. After task4 recovery, evaluate the five-triple prefix with its retained controls. If recovered task4 is a valid failure, all five remaining N32 cells would need success to reach five; a subsequent complete triple that makes five unreachable stops new work. Missing or invalid controls cannot be treated as zero, omitted to invent a complete triple, or bypassed to dispatch later tasks. Any new invalid terminal stops, retaining all charges. Promotion still needs all 30 valid cells, at least five N32 successes and matched controls; nothing here authorizes promotion, validation or formal evaluation.
