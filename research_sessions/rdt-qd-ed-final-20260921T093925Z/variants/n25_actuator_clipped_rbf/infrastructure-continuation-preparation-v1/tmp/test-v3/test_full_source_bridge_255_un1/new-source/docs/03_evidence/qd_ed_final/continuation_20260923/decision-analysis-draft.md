## Computable primary diagnostics

Let p0 be the saved native-precision source EEF position; p1…pK are the actual pre-reset positions for the selected executed prefix. Let p-hat0…p-hat8 be the selected recorded projection. Verify provider input equals p-hat1…p-hat7 exactly under the actual original H=8 contract. Let g be the reviewed frozen target. Distances use metres and the original stored values converted to float64 for arithmetic; conversion does not recover precision lost in BF16.

* EEF error: e_t = ||p_t − p-hat_t||; report the vector, endpoint error and sqrt(mean(e_t²)) over t=1…K. Separately report ||p0 − p-hat0||, the recorded origin-cast error.
* Target distance: d_t = ||p_t − g|| and d-hat_t = ||p-hat_t − g||. Actual prefix progress P_K = d0 − d_K. Predicted progress P-hat_K = d0 − d-hat_K uses the same native p0 baseline. Optimism O_K = P-hat_K − P_K. These quantities are Unknown without a resolved target; EEF error is still computable.
* Motion response gain: sum_t[(p_t−p_(t−1))·(p-hat_t−p-hat_(t−1))] / sum_t||p-hat_t−p-hat_(t−1)||². Report Unknown when predicted motion energy is at or below summed stored-projection ULP². This is descriptive tracking, not a fitted dynamics model.
* Full-eight prefixes only: R8 = d8−d7 and R-hat8 = d-hat8−d-hat7; P7 and P8; actual command signs at steps 7 and 8, their switch, and ||qpos8−qpos7|| if observed. Preserve all actual gripper/qpos/qvel prefixes; do not infer object capture from signs or joint movement.

Numerical floors are fixed from the recorded tensor dtype, not fitted to results. For a stored xyz vector, u is the L2 norm of the larger adjacent representable spacing in each coordinate using nextafter toward ±infinity, with a 1e−9 metre arithmetic floor. Distance-difference floors are the sum of the two endpoint u values. These are conservative storage-resolution screens, not measurement-noise bounds, statistical confidence intervals or a reconstruction of high precision. Use native p0 precision for progress floors. Missing or ambiguous identity, non-normal reward mode, or a change in actual translation after policy decode makes a target-based mechanistic witness ineligible; retain the observation and report that limitation separately. The source projection itself can still be compared with actual EEF descriptively.

## Hypothesis B: projected approach overstates the response of the executed action

Claim to test: selected high-quality projected approach can promise resolvable EEF progress that the corresponding real sent action does not realize. The candidate behavior/quality geometry may therefore be poorly calibrated to execution, even when QD faithfully preserves the best quality. This differs from increasing diversity, adding global-best parents, or retaining a second within-cell action: it questions the projection-to-execution correspondence used by those methods.

Primary witness for an eligible nonempty prefix: predicted P-hat_K exceeds its p0+projected-endpoint precision floor, real P_K is at or below its p0+actual-endpoint floor, and endpoint error exceeds the projected+actual endpoint floor. Report continuous O_K, RMSE, response gain, actual command/qpos series and sparse environment reward/success, including negative/non-witness cases. For a full chunk where only step8 causes the witness and steps1…7 track, prioritize A's narrower explanation rather than presenting the same event as two independent effects. A command-transform mismatch, target ambiguity or known departure objective is an attribution block, not evidence against the physical simulator or proof of collision.

A single witness is a local projection-response discrepancy. Replication across separate episodes makes a response-calibration lead worth specifying; replication across both tasks broadens that lead. If projected and actual approach track within storage resolution throughout eligible prefixes, B is unsupported here. If discrepancies exist but never turn predicted improvement into absent real improvement, report tracking error without promoting this specific hypothesis. No eligible target-aware prefix means insufficient evidence. Gripper qpos strata can locate associations; they cannot label contact, closed-grasp success or the causal origin of an error.

A potential later mechanism must use only past executed response to adjust a QD/ED geometry/feasibility representation, with a frozen causal update rule and no future-outcome access. Before proposing it, show that its observable input exists before the decision, varies meaningfully, and could change at least one recorded selection; outcome of that alternate selection remains Unknown. Do not select a numeric gain, margin or gripper threshold from these four episode outcomes, or automatically replace the provider. No new candidate is supplied or certified by this document.
