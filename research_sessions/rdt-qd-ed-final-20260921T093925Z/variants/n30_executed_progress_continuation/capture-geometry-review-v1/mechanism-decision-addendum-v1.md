**Decision remains no GPU nomination yet, because candidate-level distinction is unmeasured in this bounded evidence**, rather than because a proxy must already prove real grasping. The 32 outcomes are already-selected actions from two failures. They establish observable directional offsets, but not that available alternative candidates span useful alternative jaw-plane coordinates or that the proposed archive axis retains different candidates. The distinction from N9 is substantive on paper; it is not empirical evidence that this particular candidate supply would change. Given the remaining unprotected baseline/dev headroom, a cheaper discriminating check has priority over a GPU cohort. This is resource/evidence prioritization, not a universal prerequisite for all prospective experiments.
