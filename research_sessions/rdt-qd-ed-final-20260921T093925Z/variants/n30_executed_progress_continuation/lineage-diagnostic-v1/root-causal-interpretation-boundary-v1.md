# Root interpretation boundary: ancestry is not total causal effect

Scientific source remains6292fc8cffad2eec0f789e023d835ad3c1df146a. This is a static interpretation note, not a new data-read, replay or experimental authorization.

Two kinds of evidence must remain distinct:
- direct copied content or conventional recorded parent/offspring ancestry reaching final selected/committed actions;
- the complete causal effect of inserting a candidate into a population-coupled search.

The second cannot be decided from the first alone. In current source:
- core/rdt_policy_steer.py:5266 inserts the continuation AFTER the fresh proposal sampler. Thus the insertion cannot retroactively affect initial fresh-sampler RBF computations.
- :5441–5446 derives initial QD bounds and cell assignments from the post-insertion population.
- :5798 assigns parent source labels from allocated archive lanes; :7167 overwrites final source labels with qd_archive. Those labels do not identify original ancestors.
- :7874–7912 constructs an all-pairs inverse-distance potential and normalizes its complete gradient; :7931 uses it for the absolute_eef descriptor. Subsequent rollout ED therefore couples the particles present in that rollout. A continuation lineage can affect another particle's update without becoming its conventional parent or the final winner.

These are possible dependency paths in the code, not a claim that their values changed in either observed episode. Even a complete conventional ancestry graph can at most establish or exclude that narrow route. An ambiguous or absent direct descendant must not be relabeled as no effect on action, no utility, or justification for forcing retention. Establishing a total treatment effect would need a separately specified intervention comparison; Phase A grants none.

The finite diagnostic remains useful: it can distinguish whether copied contents/uniquely identifiable direct descendants reached selection and bind already executed feedback. It cannot establish that rejected candidates would have helped, nor that observed target approach means grasp or task success.
