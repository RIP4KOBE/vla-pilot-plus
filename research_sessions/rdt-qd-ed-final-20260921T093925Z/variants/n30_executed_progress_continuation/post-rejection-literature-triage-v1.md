This note changes no method, metric, active scope, source, budget or phase authorization. External reported performance is not evidence that a mechanism works on this RDT study.

|[KDPE, CoRL 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v305/rosasco25a.html)|Uses a kernel-density trajectory-selection strategy, with a kernel that handles Cartesian position, orientation and gripper state jointly. |[Bidirectional Decoding, ICLR 2025 / arXiv v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.17355v4)|Combines agreement with the previous chunk and forward contrast. Do not call N30 a BID reproduction or its failure a rejection of all continuity methods.|
|[Self-Guided Action Diffusion, authors' arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12189) and [project page](https://rhea-mal.github.io/selfgad.github.io/)|Guides intermediate diffusion proposals using the previous decision, with temporal weighting. Guidance gaps and stale/failed plans remain material counterevidence. No parameter, new code or experiment is selected from this paper.|
