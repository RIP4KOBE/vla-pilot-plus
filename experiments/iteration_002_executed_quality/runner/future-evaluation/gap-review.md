The evaluation builder must initially produce a nonrunnable plan with all phases false.

## Budget blocker requiring a prospective decision

Existing `check_budget` protects60 validation +40 formal episode slots from development/recovery and protects14h validation +10h formal from other buckets, within240 episode dispatches/48h. Healthchecks consume GPU-hours only. The numbers14h/10h are **protected reservations, not phase-local caps** in current code; a phase may consume unprotected global slack. The function may shorten an episode timeout near exhaustion, while current N2 Dev separately requires the full1800s. A future phase must explicitly preserve or change that policy *before* results; a shortened scientific window must not be silently represented as a matched1800s run.

At full1800s+40s reserved cleanup,60 validation processes require a worst-case30.667h;40 formal processes require20.444h, before recoveries/healthchecks. The protected14h and10h correspond to840s and900s mean total lifecycle per original cell, respectively. They do not guarantee either matrix completes. Root must preregister whether they are minimum protected allocations with preapproved global slack, or hard local caps, and the exact stop/incomplete policy. Do not reduce frozen timeout, drop controls/seeds, use a smaller denominator, release formal reserves for validation, or borrow undeclared recovery capacity to force completion. Atomic global admission always overrides a local plan; unexpected overrun may require closing a phase incomplete. Four frozen validation cohorts do not imply240 additional validation slots: the current validation bucket itself is60, so later candidates need an explicitly budget-valid future allocation, never automatic cohort reuse.
