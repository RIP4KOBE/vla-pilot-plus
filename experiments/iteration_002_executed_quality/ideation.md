Q4 Hamming: yes. Aligning the support of a search objective with actions actually sent addresses a concrete class of selection errors at unchanged search budget; test once without claiming to solve grasp or tracking.

## Four moves and selection

Assumption inversion: replace internal truncated-prefix quality with full-executed-prefix quality before archive replacement, parent search and final selection; keep raw provider quality in a separate Stage channel.

Backward from success: a controller should also retain realized progress across chunks. This suggests causal action-response feedback, but frame/timing/reset and target-identity interfaces are missing, and current evidence does not identify task-relevant failure causality. Defer this distinct stateful mechanism.

Analogical transfer: endpoint-aware receding-horizon optimization includes every command that will be committed. Apply the recognized approach objective over the complete committed horizon rather than use a post-hoc final veto. This supports the first mechanism rather than adding a second component.

Failure-case reverse engineering: task0 C0 s320 and task0 Q0 s224/s560 expose omitted-tail behavior; final-membership results show the relevant ranking must act during search and archive retention. A hard final tail veto discards old-quality winners but cannot recover earlier eliminated actions. Defer it rather than stack it with a changed objective.

Only one survivor is selected. No sweep, larger population, descriptor change, new provider or simulator branch is proposed.
