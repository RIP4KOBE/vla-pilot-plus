# Intake / diagnostic protocol

Read-only historical audit, code/profile compatibility audit, and runtime dependency inventory. No task outcome or proxy is inferred from a missing counterfactual. No GPU/model/environment dispatch. Writes limited to owned worktree and artifact root. Verify Q0/C0 historical identity against `5b0898e` and current `744e08b`; identify rather than hide differences. Separate proposal, selection, and closed-loop evidence. Build exact exposure registry before assigning validation seeds.
