# N9: contact-zone lateral-sweep selection (prospective Dev protocol)

Status: hypothesis and experiment frozen before implementation or N9 online results. Parent source is the N7 validation closure commit `28189b1`. N7 C1 was rejected at 3/20; no C1 task/seed result is used to choose this mechanism or its constants. N8 was a read-only descriptor screen, with no online result. This is a pivot from descriptor-only diversity toward executed-action safety within QD's final selection.

## Evidence and causal hypothesis

Already exposed same-task/seed Dev task 0 shows N6 succeeded and N7 failed. The N7 video shows the grasp target can toppled and the basket displaced by step 149, while the N6 video shows a more controlled early approach. Native N7 Dev candidate pools show 9/17 task-0 guided decisions and 3/10 task-3 decisions would change under the rule below; the two N7 Dev successes (tasks 6 and 9) show zero such changes in their six guided decisions. This is only a structural replay, not a counterfactual success result. The N5 grasp-proximity selector itself changed only 1/33 N7 Dev guided decisions relative to native argmin, so merely removing N5 is not a supported next hypothesis. The candidate chosen by the current scalar provider can sweep horizontally while passing near the target, making side contact and losing a stable grasp. Penalizing that geometric pattern may preserve graspable approaches without disabling QD or adding model calls.

## Single algorithm change

Keep N7's 4×4 quadrant/signed-height archive, capacity 1, parent allocation, 16×11 search, current scalar provider, Stage, reward, RDT checkpoint, perception, executor and 8-action chunk unchanged. At the final canonical 16-candidate population only, use the already computed `(16,9,3)` projected EEF positions, `(16,8,7)` decoded actions and recognized target keypoint. Preserve N5's eligible set: if any candidate has a positive gripper command at a projected position within its original absolute 0.06 m and relative 0.03 m reach limits, choose only among those; otherwise consider all 16. Preserve the original N5 selection and record a typed fallback if the target provider is unrecognized.

For each candidate `j`, mark projected position `t` as contact-zone when its XY distance to the target keypoint is `<0.08 m` and absolute height difference is `<0.08 m`. Define `sweep_j` as the sum, over eight transitions, of projected XY displacement lengths when either endpoint lies in that zone. Within the N5 eligible set, consider only candidates whose closest projected 3D reach is at most `0.02 m` worse than the original N5 selected candidate. Choose the first minimum of `native_cost_j + 0.20 * sweep_j`, in cost units per metre. The original candidate is always eligible. All constants are fixed here; no task/seed/job branch, future observation, new model call, simulator branch, or extra candidate evaluation is allowed. Report raw cost, reach and sweep changes; the modified quality semantics do not promise retention of the old raw-cost maximum.

If implemented correctly, N9 changes the final action only where lower sweep exists with comparable predicted reach, leaves descriptor/parent search active, and reduces sweep on changed Dev decisions. A structural replay is insufficient to claim task benefit.
