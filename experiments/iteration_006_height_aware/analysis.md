N6 changed the QD behavior descriptor from target-relative XY endpoint to horizontal radius plus signed endpoint height, with N5's grasp-timing final consumer and all other search/Stage/runtime conditions fixed. The frozen four original exposed Dev cells all completed on GPU3 with accepted native audits: task 0 success, tasks 3/6/9 failure, **1/4**. Same-cell historical C0/Q0 were 0/4 and N4/N5 were 1/4. The predeclared numerical gate was at least 2/4, so N6 is rejected without validation or formal release.

The descriptor mechanism activated: 3,619 native old-XY same-cell candidate pairs were separated under N6, and 5,214 later parent slots drew from cells with such a prior split. Its task-0 success is new relative to all recorded comparators on this exposed cell, but task 3 lost the N4/N5 success. These are within-sample gains and losses, not independent evidence of generalization or a causal proof about the descriptor. No rejected candidate was executed as a counterfactual. The complete cohort used four attempts and 1,533.3797815930157 GPU-process seconds; the private study totals 101/240 attempts, development 75/100 and 13.640764658837194/48 GPU hours.
