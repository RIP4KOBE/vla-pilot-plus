# N6: preserve approach height in the QD archive

Status: prospective exposed-Dev protocol, frozen before N6 implementation or outcomes. N5 was rejected at 1/4 despite seven selection changes; its task-3 success did not involve an N5 selection change. In the 55 SHA-verified N5 guided decisions, every terminal population had at least one old XY cell containing candidates whose projected endpoint heights differed by more than 0.04 m; median of the maximum same-cell height spans was 0.244140625 m. These are projection and cell-assignment facts, not evidence that the discarded height alternative would succeed. Already-executed EEF projection error was material, but a one-chunk lag correction improved only 20 of 41 adjacent pairs, so calibration is not the present mechanism.

Hypothesis: the old target-relative XY descriptor conflates different vertical approaches in a single quality-replacement cell. At fixed 16-cell capacity, grouping candidates by horizontal target distance and signed target-relative height may retain an approach that better supports grasping in the final population. The failure mode may instead be grasp orientation, projector error, target-keypoint mismatch or closed-loop execution; the Dev outcomes can falsify task benefit.

Make exactly one primary algorithm change relative to N5: for each already projected candidate at the native eighth action, replace the two QD descriptor axes `(endpoint_x-target_x, endpoint_y-target_y)` with `(sqrt((endpoint_x-target_x)^2+(endpoint_y-target_y)^2), endpoint_z-target_z)`, both float32 in metres. Use the same current-observation keypoint-0 target that the old descriptor used; all presently exposed guided N5 provider templates are source-bound to index 0. The radial coordinate is nonnegative and signed height preserves above/below distinction. Keep the 4x4 grid, 16 cells, capacity one, bounds rule, Q0 parent sampling/replacement, original provider reward and costs, 16-by-10 ED schedule, exact 176 evaluations/11 score calls, N5 proximity-gated final consumer, Stage, checkpoint, action execution and all other settings unchanged. No additional projector, model, reward, VLM or simulator call. Record native projected endpoint, descriptor, cell assignment, parent/replacement and actual selected/decoded/dispatch identities. Fail closed on nonfinite, shape/dtype or association violations; the old XY mode must regress unchanged.
